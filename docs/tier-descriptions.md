# Audited v2 scope

| Tier | Core tasks | Scientific design |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 6 | One-sample, paired and pooled two-sample t tests; proportion precision |
| 2 | 6 | Fixed Gaussian regression, one-way ANOVA and factorial effect tests |
| 3 | 4 | Repeated Gaussian measurements with specified correlations and sphericity |
| 4 | 4 | Riley prediction-development sample-size criteria |

The tiers organize method families; they are not empirically calibrated difficulty levels. The core is a restricted 20-task execution benchmark, not certification of the 106 legacy tasks or all methods in the original tier descriptions. A separately frozen source extension adds Freedman survival and cluster-rate examples. No deterministic-core score supports a stochastic mixed-model accuracy claim. See [the protocol](audited-methodology.md). Legacy descriptions are archived under `historical/docs/`.
